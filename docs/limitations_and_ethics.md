# FRAUDNET AI — Limitations, Ethical Considerations & Forensic Disclaimers

## 1. Academic Scope & Forensic Disclaimer

FRAUDNET AI is an advanced research and academic intelligence system developed using the publicly released **Elliptic Bitcoin transaction dataset**.

> **IMPORTANT FORENSIC NOTICE:**
> Risk probabilities, graph centrality metrics, and typology classifications generated by FRAUDNET AI are statistical indicators designed to assist human AML investigators. They **do not constitute automated proof of legal wrongdoing, criminal intent, or financial guilt**. All findings require independent blockchain ledger verification, counterparty identification, and human compliance review before initiating legal proceedings or freezing assets.

## 2. Technical & Methodological Limitations

### 2.1 Pseudo-Anonymity of Bitcoin
- The Elliptic dataset abstracts Bitcoin wallet addresses into transaction identifiers (`txId`).
- A high fan-in transaction may indicate money-laundering aggregation, but it may equally represent benign, protocol-level behaviors such as mining pool reward consolidations, multi-signature corporate treasury operations, or cryptocurrency exchange sweep wallets.

### 2.2 Unlabeled Transaction Volume (Unknowns)
- **77.1% of transactions** in the dataset ($157,205$ out of $203,769$) carry the `unknown` label.
- While FRAUDNET AI strictly prevents unknown transactions from contaminating the supervised training ground truth, unseen illicit transactions within the unknown pool can distort false positive calculations.

### 2.3 Feature Obfuscation
- The original 165 numerical features provided in the Elliptic dataset were normalized and anonymized by the original dataset authors (features 1–93 local, 94–165 aggregated).
- Although our local Z-score attribution engine identifies statistical anomalies relative to the licit baseline, exact real-world Bitcoin transaction attributes (such as lock times or specific OP_RETURN payloads) are abstracted.

## 3. Ethical Considerations in Financial AI

### 3.1 Mitigation of De-Banking & False Positives
In automated AML systems, false positive flags can lead to unlawful financial exclusion, frozen bank accounts, and delayed legitimate transactions.
- FRAUDNET AI combats this by providing **transparent, local feature attributions (Z-scores)** and multi-hop counterparty context rather than opaque black-box scores.
- Risk scores are partitioned into four distinct action tiers, ensuring that low and moderate risk transactions are never subjected to automated asset freezes.

### 3.2 Human-in-the-Loop Governance
FRAUDNET AI is explicitly engineered as an investigative co-pilot, not an autonomous decider:
- The **Case Management** module requires named compliance officers to record findings and log notes.
- The **AI Forensic Assistant** cites concrete evidence, graph degrees, and confidence intervals to ensure human oversight.

## 4. Responsible AI Principles Applied
1. **Reproducibility:** Fully containerized virtual environment with locked dependency versions and deterministic seeds.
2. **Accountability:** SQLite audit trails record case updates, attached suspect transactions, and report generation timestamps.
3. **Transparency:** Modular codebase with comprehensive test suites (109/109 tests passing) and open documentation.
